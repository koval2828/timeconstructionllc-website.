Our approach

Quality starts before the first tile.

Time Construction LLC brings more than 20 years of construction experience to tile, stone and flooring work. We focus on communication, careful preparation and clean results.

  1. Listen. Understand your vision and requirements.
  2. Plan. Discuss materials, layout and a written estimate.
  3. Prepare. Pay attention to substrates and waterproofing.
  4. Finish. Deliver precise lines and thoughtful details.
  5. Review. Walk through the completed installation and discuss care and maintenance.

Common questions

Before your project begins.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. On-site consultations and written estimates are free within Anchorage.

Do you work outside Anchorage?

Yes, in surrounding areas. A travel fee may apply depending on distance, and will be disclosed and agreed in advance. If you proceed, it is credited toward the project price.

What should I prepare?

Tell us about your project location, desired materials, approximate dimensions and timeline. Photos are helpful, but not required for an initial conversation.

Do you install waterproofing?

Yes. Shower preparation and waterproofing are central parts of our tile work. We use suitable systems including Wedi, GoBoard and Schluter.

Can you install heated tile floors?

Yes. We install electric floor-heating systems as part of tile projects, including suitable Schluter systems.

Can you work with my materials?

We can discuss materials you have selected and check their suitability for the planned installation during consultation.
