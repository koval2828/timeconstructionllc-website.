Common questions

Before your project begins.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. On-site consultations and written estimates are free within Anchorage.

Do you work outside Anchorage?

Yes, in surrounding areas. A travel fee may apply depending on distance, and will be disclosed and agreed in advance. If you proceed, it is credited toward the project price.

What should I prepare?

Tell us about your project location, desired materials, approximate dimensions and timeline. Photos are helpful, but not required for an initial conversation.
